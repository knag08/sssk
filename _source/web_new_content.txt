

In January 2017, Bhagavan Sri Sathya Sai Baba instructed Mata Vishwatmananda Giri—through direct conversation—to construct "Sri Sathya Sai Kutir." He stated that this location would serve as a center of spiritual practice (*sadhana*) for thousands of seekers and that Lord Panchamukhi Anjaneya Swamy is the guardian deity (*Kshetrapalaka*) of this sacred site.

The message given by the guardian deity, Anjaneya Swamy, regarding Sri Sathya Sai Kutir is as follows:

"I, who maintain that every action in life is a form of spiritual practice, urge you all to build these *Kutirs* (cottages). Through public service, spiritual teachings, and collective spiritual practices, strive to pave the way for a noble life defined by sacrifice and to establish peace and happiness in society. In a sense, this is not merely an *Ashram* (hermitage), but a true *Aashrayam* (sanctuary/refuge)!

I am the very center and source of strength for the *Annapurnalaya* (dining hall), the *Mandir* (temple), and all other facilities here. This place is not just a father’s lap or a sanctum of affection; it is also a great school of equality and love.

If, upon leaving Me, you were to say, 'Swami, I could not stay with You this time,' the answer from Me—without a moment's hesitation—must be: 'You *were* with Me all along; you were constantly chanting My Name.' You must come to Puttaparthi and engage in spiritual practice within the *Kutir* in such a way that this truth is realized. Whenever you contemplate My Name in your mind, you attain My presence.

I am seated here in these *Kutirs*, fully prepared to serve you the bounty of grace." Transcending the boundaries of time and space, I wish to bring fulfillment to the lives of so many people here. Experience contentment, peace, and joy in My divine presence. I, Anjaneya—whose resolve is unfailing—offer this opportunity to you all and grant you the courage to make the most of it.

**Inauguration of the Kuteer**

Through Jnani Bele, Bhagavan Baba facilitated the mobilization of financial resources for the construction. He provided guidance on various aspects—such as land acquisition, the *Bhoomi Puja* (ground-breaking ceremony), the architectural style, and the selection of the builder. Consequently, the "Sri Sathya Sai Kuteer" was inaugurated on December 24, 2017, coinciding with the Christmas festival.

**Activities at the Kuteer**

Activity Details

Abhishekam:
A 15-minute Abhishekam is performed daily for the Salagrama, accompanied by the chanting of Abhisheka mantras and Shiva Stotras.

Geetha Homam:
The Geetha Homam takes place daily at 9:00 AM on the premises of the Sri Sathya Sai Kutir. As part of this program, the fire ritual is conducted for approximately 45 minutes, featuring the chanting of Daivagna Hotra mantras, one chapter of the Bhagavad Gita, and the Gayatri mantras of various deities. Anyone is welcome to participate in this program.

Nitya Narayana Seva (Daily Service to the Divine in Humanity):
Here is a true incident:
A man brought an elderly woman to an old-age home and said, "She has lived in our street for a long time; she is destitute (an orphan), and I brought her here hoping you could provide her shelter." While he was speaking, a friend accompanying him looked at him and asked, "Did you bring your own mother to admit her to this old-age home?" We were left speechless. There are so many children today who not only act irresponsibly towards their own parents but also reduce them to a state of destitution. The 'Nitya Narayana Seva' (Daily Service to the Divine in the needy) program was initiated with the aim of sharing love, care, trust, and courage with elderly individuals experiencing loneliness—qualities that may have been lost from their lives. Beyond merely providing daily meals, we regularly inquire about their well-being and strive to resolve their problems to the best of our ability. To meet their needs, Bhagavan Baba guides this program miraculously; with immense love and in ways beyond our imagination, He channels the grace-filled power and wisdom required to sustain it. 
Elderly individuals in need of such service are identified by visiting streets in Puttaparthi and six surrounding villages and gathering details from local residents. Meals are cooked daily at 'Sri Sathya Sai Kutir' and distributed in steel carriers to approximately 50 elderly individuals (referred to as 'Narayanas') for both lunch and dinner.

Monthly Narayana Seva:
Commemorating Bhagavan Baba’s birthday, 'Anna Prasadam' (sanctified food) has been served every month on the 23rd at Sri Sathya Sai Kutir since December 2017. What began with 76 individuals has now grown to serve approximately 1,000 'Narayanas'. Medical Services:
During the last week of every month, medical check-ups are conducted by expert doctors for approximately 20 elderly individuals in need, and the necessary medicines are provided to them. 
A true incident illustrating the magnitude of Bhagavan Baba's love for the destitute.
