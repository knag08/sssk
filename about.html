About Us

Sri Sathya Sai Kuteer is a spiritual center located in Puttaparthi, Sathya Sai district, AP, INDIA. This being a public charitable trust provides a platform for spiritual aspirants in pursuing spiritual growth through various spiritual activities.

Under the divine guidance of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba, through Mata Vishwatmananda Giri various spiritual and service activities have been set up to achieve individual Spiritual enlightenment.
