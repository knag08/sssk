Clean water is a right, not a purchase. Yet in and around Puttaparthi many families simply cannot afford safe drinking water month after month.

A **reverse-osmosis plant** on the Kuteer premises runs continuously, and what it produces is given away. There is no counter, no queue-jumping and no closing time — the service is open **24 hours a day, seven days a week**, so that a family that works late is not a family that goes without.

## How it works

- **RO purification.** Water is treated to remove impurities and contaminants, then stored in clean tanks and drawn off at the collection point.
- **A card for every family.** Each household holds a card entitling it to **20 litres a day**. It keeps distribution fair and cuts waste, without anyone having to ask permission.
- **Kept clean.** Tanks, filters and the collection area are monitored and cleaned on a regular schedule. Water given in charity must still be water you would drink yourself.

More than **1,000 families** now depend on it. In the financial year 2025–26 the plant gave out over **84.6 lakh litres**.
