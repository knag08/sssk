Healthcare and education build a community together. Around **40 children from classes 1 to 9** come to the Kuteer six evenings a week, Monday through Saturday, for tuition with professional tutors — help with the subjects they are struggling with, and the confidence that comes from finally understanding.

Every child is given an evening snack before the session begins. A hungry child cannot concentrate, and no child should have to.

## Beyond the syllabus

- **Computer awareness** — a weekly class for students of grades 8 and 9, building the digital skills their schools cannot yet offer.
- **Yoga** — Sunday sessions in traditional practice, for physical steadiness and mental calm.
- **Learning outside the classroom** — excursions that show children what their studies are actually for.

## The village tuition centres

The Kuteer also runs tuition where the children are, rather than asking them to travel.

- In **Narsimpalli** village, teachers we employ take tuition for around **100 government-school children** of classes 1 to 8, six days a week.
- In **Narsimpalli Thanda**, the same is done for **20 children** of classes 1 to 5, six days a week.

These are ordinary government-school pupils whose families cannot pay for coaching, in villages where none is on offer at any price.

## The Bangalore excursion

Education is not only academics; it is opening doors to what a child did not know existed. We took our students to Bangalore, to the **Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium**, the **Visvesvaraya Industrial and Technological Museum**, **Cubbon Park** and the aquarium.

They came back with questions we could not answer. That was the point.
