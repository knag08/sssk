A cooked meal answers today. A month's provisions answer the weeks after.

As part of our community work, kits of essential groceries are given to selected households in and around Puttaparthi — families identified through the same street-by-street visits that guide our daily Narayana Seva, and through neighbours who tell us quietly who is struggling.

The provisions ease an immediate pressure. Just as much, the visit itself says something a food packet cannot: *you have not been overlooked.*
